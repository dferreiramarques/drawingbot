- ems-readability.json, ems-osmotron.json, ems-nixish.json, ems-allure.json, ems-tech.json

  conversão SVG de Windell H. Oskay, sob a SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). São derivadas,

  (Jovanny Lemonad), Allura (TypeSETit) e Architects Daughter (Kimberly Geswein).
- hershey-serif.json, hershey-serif-italic.json, hershey-sans.json
  Fontes Hershey, criadas pelo Dr. A. V. Hershey no U.S. National Bureau of Standards, convertidas
  em SVG por Windell H. Oskay (Evil Mad Scientist Laboratories) a partir de emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey.
